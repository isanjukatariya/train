Overview

Finding a safe stay near campus, made simple.

Dormi is a PG and hostel finder built for students who move to a new city and need a place to live near campus. It replaces broker calls and unverified listings with clear filters, honest photos, and transparent rent details.

Verified

Every listing shows real photos and clear house rules.

Mapped

Compared

Rent, food and facilities side by side.

The Problem

Scattered listings & unverified stays

Students arriving in a new city depend on brokers, word of mouth, and social media groups to find a PG or hostel. Listings are often outdated, photos don't match reality, and rent, deposits and house rules aren't clear until it's too late.

The Solution

One place to search, compare & shortlist

A student-first finder with filters for budget, distance and facilities, detailed listing pages with real photos and house rules, and a simple shortlist to compare stays side by side.

How We Solved It

A visual walkthrough of the product experience.

Takeaway

Designing for trust.

Dormi taught me that when someone is choosing a place to live, design has to build trust before it builds delight. Clear information, honest visuals and no surprises matter more than anything flashy.
