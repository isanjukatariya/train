Overview

Bridging the language gap for frontline workers.

WorkMate is a multilingual workforce management app designed to help frontline workers access job-related information, manage daily tasks, and communicate easily in their preferred regional language—eliminating confusion and empowering independent work.

-80%

Visual

Icon-driven interface designed for varying literacy levels.

The Problem

Language barriers & text-heavy portals

Frontline workers often struggle with complex, English-first management portals. This leads to missed tasks, payroll confusion, and a heavy reliance on supervisors to verbally translate and explain daily duties.

The Solution

1-Tap localization & visual checklists

An accessible, highly visual mobile interface that allows workers to seamlessly switch to their native language, view icon-supported task lists, and resolve queries without supervisor intervention.

How We Solved It

A visual walkthrough of the product experience.

Takeaway

Clarity over cleverness.

Designing WorkMate was an exercise in extreme empathy. It reinforced that good UI isn't about complex animations or trendy layouts—it's about stripping away cognitive load so anyone, regardless of tech literacy or language, can do their job with confidence.
